# Surviving Lagos Island: Street Level

A first-person zombie survival game set on the streets of Lagos, Nigeria. Pick a survivor, or take your chances in the danfo, and hold the road from Lagos Island to the last flight out of Ikeja.

**Play in your browser:** https://alternatefact.github.io/surviving-lagos-island/

Works on phones (turn sideways) and computers. No download or sign-up.
